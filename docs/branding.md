> Use case: logo-brand. Create a large square PNG app icon for PrintWatcher, a 3D printer monitoring web app. A stylized human eye silhouette with a 3D printer hotend/nozzle forming the iris and pupil, clear recognizable eye with pointed brass nozzle centered inside. Bold clean geometric graphic, strong dark outline, teal eye and warm brass nozzle, tasteful dark navy rounded-square backdrop, centered generous padding, legible at favicon size. No letters, text, watermark, gradients or tiny details. High resolution 1024x1024 or larger.
